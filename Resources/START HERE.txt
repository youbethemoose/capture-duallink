CAPTURE DUALLINK
Two views. One composition.

THE IDEA
Photographer: keep Capture One's original Live View on the laptop. Adjust focus,
add a grid or overlay, and direct where the product should move.
Prop stylist: watch the same live frame on the iPad while placing the product.

INSTALL
Drag Capture DualLink.app to Applications. Eject the disk image and open the app.
The Setup Guide opens the first time. The linked shutter is in the Dock and menu
bar; click either to reopen the window picker. Closing the picker keeps the app
running. To keep its Dock icon after quitting, right-click it > Options > Keep in Dock.
Requires macOS 14 or later; supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

SET UP
1. Connect the iPad with Sidecar, using the same Apple Account on both devices.
   In System Settings > Displays, choose an extended display, not mirroring.
2. Open DualLink and allow it in Screen Recording settings. Reopen if asked.
3. In Capture One, choose Window > Live View. Select it in DualLink and click
   Duplicate Window. Keep the original on the laptop; move the copy to the iPad.
   Start with Native / Retina and 30 fps.

ON SET
- Adjust focus in Capture One, if your connected camera and lens support it.
- Add a grid or reference overlay in Capture One to show it on both views.
  DualLink's own Grid affects only its copy.
- Hover for controls. Click Control to use the mouse in the original window.
  Allow DualLink in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility.
  Clicks, drags and scrolling then reach Capture One; the cursor stays on the copy.
- Use Fill Display in the floating toolbar to fill the iPad. Double-click also
  fills/exits in view-only mode; with Control On it goes to Capture One instead.
- Use the floating toolbar's three dots for Trim, Fit and more copies.
  Reset Trim to bring back source buttons you previously cropped out.
- Separate source menus/dialogs aren't included in a single window's copy.
  Focus and zoom behavior depend on the original app, camera and lens.
- Hide Sidecar's sidebar and Touch Bar for more viewing space. If finger controls
  aren't available, use Apple Pencil or your Mac's mouse.
- Use Capture One on a calibrated display for critical color decisions.

IF THE VIEW IS BLANK
Restore the original Live View window. Check capture permission and refresh the
source list. A closed source must be selected again. Reopen Setup Guide anytime.
Sidecar supports one iPad; other screens use your Mac's display connections.

BEFORE THE SHOOT
Try your actual camera, Mac and iPad together. Confirm both views update while
moving a product, test focus in Capture One, and check a shared grid or overlay.
Disconnect and reconnect the iPad once so you know the recovery steps on your kit.

OPTIONAL AUTO-START
Shutter icon > Automatic Startup lets you open DualLink away from home Wi-Fi.
This is off on a fresh install. It needs Location permission for Wi-Fi names.
Your home network name stays on your Mac. Quit stops the watcher until relaunch
or login. Disconnected Wi-Fi does not count as away.
